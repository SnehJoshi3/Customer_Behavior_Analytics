# Customer Behavior & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI analytics project exploring customer purchasing patterns, category performance, subscription adoption, and age-group trends across a retail customer dataset.

---

## Project Overview

* **Dataset Scale:** 3.9K customer records.
* **Key KPIs Tracked:** Number of Customers (`3.9K`), Average Purchase Amount (`$59.76`), and Average Review Rating (`3.75`)
* **Tools Used:** Microsoft Power BI (interactive report, slicers, KPI cards, and data visualizations).

---

## Dashboard Features & Interactive Filters

The report layout pairs left-hand interactive slicers with high-level summary metrics at the top and granular breakdowns across the canvas:

* **Interactive Filters:** Filter dynamically by **Gender** (`Female`, `Male`), **Subscription Status** (`No`, `Yes`), **Category** (`Accessories`, `Footwear`, `Clothing`, `Outerwear`), and **Shipping Type** (`2-Day Shipping`, `Express`, `Free Shipping`, `Next Day Air`, `Standard`, `Store Pickup`).
* **Subscription Breakdown:** Donut chart comparing customer share by subscription status.
* **Category Performance:** Column charts comparing **Revenue By Category** and **Sales By Category** across product lines.
* **Age Group Analysis:** Horizontal bar charts showing **Revenue By Age Group** and **Sales By Age Group** across `Young Adult`, `Middle-aged`, `Adult`, and `Senior` segments.

---

## Key Insights

* **Subscription Gap:** **73%** of customers are non-subscribers compared to **27%** with active subscriptions, pointing to an opportunity for conversion or loyalty campaigns.
* **Top Revenue & Sales Drivers:** **Clothing** leads both revenue (**$104K**) and sales volume (**1,737**), followed by **Accessories** (**$74K** revenue; **1,240** sales).
* **Age Segment Patterns:** **Young Adult** generates the highest revenue (**$62K**) and sales count (**1,028**), closely followed by **Middle-aged** (**$59K**; **986** sales).
